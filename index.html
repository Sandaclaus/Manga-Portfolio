Hello! I’m Snow Parfait — welcome to my personal book collection.
What you see here is a curated selection of my works that I’ve managed to recover, archive, and translate into English for a smooth reading experience. The original pieces were handwritten in Thai (that can be a bit tricky to read). While I have a deep love for traditional manuscript-making, I’ve recently been exploring the digital realm.
In my older works, you might spot a few smudges or lower-resolution details. Those were originally drawn by hand on small A5 paper, a stark contrast to the high-resolution B5 format (350 DPI) I use digitally today. Thank you for stopping by, and I hope you enjoy reading!
